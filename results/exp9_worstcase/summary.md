# exp9_worstcase

E9 worst-case adversary AW (exp1_main setting). For every honest camera, round and method separately, the Byzantine neighbours send the messages from C (full confidence at each level, low confidence at each level, silence) that maximise the error of the output of the camera, knowing the rule, the truth and the honest messages (bflood.sim.aw_search). Exact for stateless rules; a lower bound on the damage for bf_v1 (stateful).
